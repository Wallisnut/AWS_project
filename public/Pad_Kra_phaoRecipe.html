Pad Kraprow

Level : Easy

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Cut pork shoulder into pieces and mince finely to desired texture, then set aside.
  2. Pound dried chilies until fine, then add fresh Jinda chilies and garlic, pounding until smooth (or blend if preferred).
  3. Heat a pan over medium heat, add a moderate amount of vegetable oil, and stir-fry the chili-garlic paste until fragrant.
  4. Once fragrant, add minced pork, stirring to break it apart and prevent clumping. Stir-fry until the pork is partially cooked and well-mixed with the paste.
  5. Season with oyster sauce, fish sauce, seasoning sauce, sweet dark soy sauce (if using), and sugar. Increase heat and stir-fry until well-combined.
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning, then add holy basil leaves and stir-fry until wilted.
  7. Turn off the heat, transfer to a plate, and serve.
